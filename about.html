About Me

Background, skills, languages, and a bit of who I am outside of research.

Background

I'm Andrew Phillip D. Aviado, a second-year undergraduate at Washington University in St. Louis (Class of 2028) pursuing a B.A. in Data Science and Public Health & Society, with a minor in Biomedical Data Science. I came to WashU on a Full-Ride Kessler Scholarship.

Academic Focus

My coursework spans statistics, algorithms, spatial analysis, and data management — giving me both theoretical foundations and applied skills. I'm drawn to problems where messy, real-world data meets meaningful human outcomes: accelerating climate simulations, improving stroke recovery assessment, or building data infrastructure for medical labs.

Research Philosophy

Every model I build, I ask: who does this help, and how do I explain why it works? That mindset carries into how I write code, design interfaces, and collaborate across disciplines.

Beyond the Lab

I currently serve as Secretary of PUSO (Philippine United Student Organization) and am the incoming President — supporting 75+ members across the Filipino and Filipino-American community at WashU. I'm also the Choreographer for the Tinikling Act at WashU's Lunar New Year Festival — a traditional Filipino folk dance performed with bamboo poles.

Outside academics I love playing guitar, singing, cycling around Forest Park, going to the gym, and cooking. I speak four languages, which shapes how I think about communication and the communities my research should serve.

Community Service

Back home I participated in the Rotary Club of Bacolod North's 23rd Youth Camp, engaging in leadership activities for improving education in public schools in Bacolod City. I also donated goods and helped clean debris after Typhoon Odette.
