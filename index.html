Research Interests

My work sits at the crossroads of data science, AI, and real-world impact:

AI in Public Health

ML models for disease outcomes, healthcare access, and population-level interventions.

Machine Learning & Deep Learning

Time-series analysis, PCA, uncertainty quantification, and generative AI for science.

Climate & Environmental Science

Physics-informed generative AI for climate modeling; paleoclimate ML methods.

Neuroscience & Imaging

U-Net ML segmentation, stroke recovery behavioral analysis, and resting-state neural imaging.

Human-Computer Interaction

Accessible, user-centered interfaces bridging complex data and everyday decision-making.

Big Data & GIS

Spatial data analysis, geospatial visualization, and large-scale data management.
