  1. Summer 2026 WashU DI²
    St. Louis, MO

    Digital Transformation Corps (DTRC) Research Intern ▶ CURRENT

    WashU DI² Accelerator · 10-Week Paid Fellowship

    Project: Quantifying Aerosol Effects on Clouds using Physics-Informed Generative AI

    • Fine-tuning physics-informed generative AI models to simulate counterfactual cloud states — predicting how marine low-level clouds would appear under different aerosol concentrations while holding meteorology constant.
    • Applying causal inference and language modeling techniques to isolate aerosol semi-direct effects from natural atmospheric variation.
    • Translating natural-language instructions into scientific workflows, building foundations for enhanced climate projections.

    Faculty: Prof. Bo Li · Prof. Jiang Wang · Prof. Mengxin (Maxine) Yu
